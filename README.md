# 🎓 Analyze TA Help Sessions at Kelley (Power BI Project)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes **Teaching Assistant (TA) help session data** within the Kelley School of Business using Power BI. The goal is to uncover scheduling trends, TA utilization patterns, student appointment behavior, and lead-time efficiency, enabling data-driven improvements in student academic support.

The project was completed as part of the Cloud-Based Analytics course, using **interactive Power BI dashboards** across multiple analysis sheets.

## 📁 Data Insights & Dashboard Sheets

### 📅 Sheet 1: Appointment Availability
- **Tools Used**: Slicer, Matrix, Stacked Column, Card
- **Key Insights**:
  - Tuesdays are the busiest for TA appointments.
  - Peak slots: 12 PM – 4 PM (especially 3 PM).
  - Many slots remain unbooked → scope for scheduling improvement.

### 👤 Sheet 2: TA's Booked
- **Tools Used**: Stacked Bar, Slicer, Clustered Bar
- **Key Insights**:
  - Kevin and Angela have the highest bookings; Anne the lowest.
  - Monday sees the most student bookings; Friday the least.

### ☀️ Sheet 3: Day Segment Insights
- **Tools Used**: Stacked Bar, Sunburst Chart
- **Key Insights**:
  - Afternoon sessions are most in demand.
  - TAs show clear time-of-day preferences.

### 📆 Sheet 4: Weekly Appointment Trends
- **Tools Used**: Pie Chart, Line Chart, Area Chart
- **Key Insights**:
  - Week 9 has the highest appointment activity.
  - Cumulative appointments show peak periods toward mid-semester.

### ⏳ Sheet 5: Lead Time Insights
- **Tools Used**: Slicer, Pie Chart, Stacked Column
- **Key Insights**:
  - Most students book appointments a few days in advance.
  - Visualization of lead-time helps forecast peak traffic.

### ⏱️ Sheet 6: Lead Hour Insights
- **Tools Used**: Funnel Chart, Gauge
- **Key Insights**:
  - Measures average lead hours per day.
  - Indicates real-time appointment behavior and TA scheduling efficiency.

## 📊 Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI  
- Data Slicers, Matrix Tables, Cards  
- Visualizations: Pie Charts, Bar Graphs, Sunburst, Funnel, Area, Line, Gauge

## ✅ Key Recommendations

1. **Track long-term academic impact** of TA sessions on grades.
2. **Collect and analyze student feedback** to improve session value.
3. **Merge appointment data with curriculum gaps** for targeted TA support.
4. **Calculate TA utilization metrics** to optimize workload and schedules.
5. **Benchmark against peer institutions** to adopt best practices.

## 📈 Outcome

The Power BI dashboards helped Kelley understand TA scheduling efficiency, student preferences, peak hours, and resource gaps. Insights from the visualizations support data-driven decision-making to enhance TA allocation and student academic outcomes.
